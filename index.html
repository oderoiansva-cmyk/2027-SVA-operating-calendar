Reset

The Quarterly Reset Protocol

The Reset Is the Work The reset is not a break from the work. It is the work that makes the next quarter possible. During Weeks 12, 24, 36 and 46, stop the standard weekly rhythm and run the five steps below.
Step Action
1Export the quarter’s Mail Suite data.
2Tier all 240 prospects: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
3Review pipeline, conversion, cash, risks.
4Set the next quarter’s strategy: target segments, cohort composition, any adjustments.
5Prepare the next quarter’s cohort list.
